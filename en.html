About me

I have developed my experience in the automotive and metalworking sectors, supporting companies and industrial groups to which I direct my professional services.

What I do

Management control and financial direction

Budgeting, performance analysis, liquidity management, cash flow, relations with banks and financial institutions, management reporting. CFO functions for groups with international presence.

Financial statements, consolidations and audit

Financial statements under Italian OIC principles, consolidated accounts, statutory audit under ISA Italia. Roles as statutory auditor and legal auditor.

Corporate advisory and extraordinary transactions

Extraordinary transactions as advisor (M&A, reorganizations, mergers, demergers), governance and administrative and corporate coordination.

Corporate taxation

Preparation and filing of tax returns, corporate and administrative fulfillments, direct taxes and VAT including cross-border transactions.

How I work

I work directly with the entrepreneur and management. I start from the company data, verify it and turn it into clear information for decision-making. I personally follow the assignments and coordinate, when necessary, the other professionals involved.

I use structured calculation models, management dashboards and analysis tools (including Business Intelligence and artificial intelligence) to make reporting timely and verifiable.
