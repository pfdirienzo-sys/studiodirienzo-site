About me

I am a Chartered Accountant and Statutory Auditor. Based in Umbria, I operate in Italy and worldwide, either in person or through the professional networks built over more than 35 years of activity (since the early 1990s). I work in Italian, English, Spanish and French.

I developed my experience mainly in the automotive, metalworking and industrial sectors. My services are therefore focused on this type of companies and groups.

I act as CFO of an industrial metalworking group in the automotive sector with subsidiaries and affiliates in Italy and abroad. I also serve as statutory auditor and legal auditor in capital companies, both industrial and real estate.

I work directly with the entrepreneur and management: personal contact, without intermediate filters. I go to the clients, operate wherever needed and use advanced tools and technologies to deliver concrete and timely results.

What I do

Management control and CFO

Budgeting, performance analysis, liquidity management, cash flow, group consolidations and management reporting. CFO functions for industrial groups with international presence.

Financial statements and audit

Financial statements under Italian OIC principles, consolidated accounts, statutory audit under ISA Italia. Roles as statutory auditor and legal auditor.

Corporate taxation

Direct taxes and VAT, including cross-border transactions. Tax litigation and relations with the tax authorities.

Corporate law and governance

Extraordinary transactions, governance, intercompany relations and administrative and corporate coordination.

Financial planning

Relations with banks and financial institutions, financial planning and management of liquidity and financing issues.

Tools and reporting

Structured Excel models, reporting systems, management dashboards, Business Intelligence and AI tools for analysis and reporting.

How I work

The approach is concrete and decision-oriented: analysis of the numbers, verifiable calculation models, orderly documentation and precise regulatory references. I use professional consolidation tools and structured Excel models; I develop management dashboards and integrate artificial intelligence solutions to speed up analysis and reporting.

For the company I am the single point of contact with banks and financial institutions, the tax authorities, notaries, auditors and foreign advisors. I coordinate information flows and support the entrepreneur and management from the preliminary phase to execution.
